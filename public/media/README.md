# Media Folder for LensVault

Place your personal files here to display them in your vault:

- `public/media/images/` -> Put your photos & images here (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF)
- `public/media/videos/` -> Put your video files here (MP4, WEBM, MOV)
- `public/media/audio/`  -> Put your music & audio files here (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC)

Once you put any file into these folders, they can be referenced in your vault as:
- `/media/images/your-photo.jpg`
- `/media/videos/your-video.mp4`
- `/media/audio/your-song.mp3`
